03 — How the machine finds a map

Autoresearch, in four moves

01

Parametrize

A projection is written as two symmetric polynomials in longitude & latitude — 18 free numbers. Equirectangular is one point in this space; no named projection sits exactly on any other.

02

Measure

At every point we take Tissot's indicatrix — the two principal scale factors a,b. Shape error is ⟨ln(a/b)²⟩; area error is the variance of ln(a·b).

03

Hill-climb

Adam gradient descent minimizes a weighted blend of the two errors, with a barrier that forbids the map from folding over itself.

04

Sweep

Vary the blend weight and the optimum traces a whole Pareto frontier — a continuum of new projections, each best-in-class for its own shape/area trade.

The human-in-the-loop objective
